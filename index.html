Dzhanibekov effect simulation

Spring 2025 · Python

A 3D physics simulation of the intermediate axis theorem in zero gravity. It is the animation running at the top of this page.

  • Real-time matrix transformations keep the orientation exact through each axis flip.
  • Energy tracking verifies that the physics stays accurate during the flips.
